Who We Are

Lootly began as a small corner shop with a big dream — to bring together the most exciting, high-quality toys and collectibles under one roof. What started with a handful of scale models has grown into the most beloved toy destination.

We believe collecting should feel like an adventure. Every shelf is stocked with intention, every product earns its place, and every visit should leave you with something special.

What We Stand For

Quality over quantity. Passion over profit. Our team are collectors first, and we personally test and curate everything we stock. If we wouldn't be proud to put it in our own collection, it doesn't make the cut.

From rare 1:64 diecasts to premium action figures, we source from the world's best manufacturers — and we're always on the hunt for the next great find.
